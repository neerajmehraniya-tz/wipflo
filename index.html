Focus page · design exploration

Focus page variations

Three interactive versions of the Focus list. Each opens as its own page, and you can switch between them from the control in the bottom-right corner of any design.

Design 1

Quick filters + status tabs
  • Quick filters as text chips. Selected shows a close icon, hover highlights.
  • 7 filters upfront with “Show more” / “Show less”.
  • Status filter as a tab strip with coloured counts.
  • Sort arrows always visible on every column.
Open Design 1

Design 2

Workspaces + pill filters
  • Workspace switcher in the left navigation.
  • Pill quick filters. Active is filled, hover darkens the border.
  • “+4 more” expands to all and turns into “Show less”.
  • Sort arrow appears on hover for unsorted columns.
Open Design 2

Design 3

Saved views
  • Workspace switcher and grouped navigation.
  • Quick filters replaced by a saved views selector.
  • “Save View” appears only after a filter or status changes.
  • Sort arrow appears on hover for unsorted columns.
Open Design 3

In every design

These interactions work the same way across all three.

  • Status filter: pick several statuses at once, or All to reset.
  • Column resize: drag the line between headers. It turns blue on hover.
  • Row selection: checkboxes per row and a select-all in the header.
  • Bulk actions: Complete, Edit and Publish appear at the bottom once rows are selected.
  • Sorting: click a header to sort, click again to reverse.
  • Search and Filters: search by workstream or client, filter by priority, department or manager.
